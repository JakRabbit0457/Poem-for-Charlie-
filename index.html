"Sunrise" 

Beneath the gleam of a sunrise. 
A radiant light pressing through a dirty window,  
She rested her head to the side embraced by the brilliant glow.

The watercolor eyes looked from afar then, 
peering over at the window, 
at her, 
beneath the lambent sun. 

In the boy's eyes across the aisle,
the soft amber light seemed around her an incandescent portent.
Gentle and streaming, 
a synchronicity which pressed in his mind and heart. 

The shining rays poked at his gazing eyes,
and in that moment the sun seemed to surround her and say:  
"Hey, look at her, stupid boy!" 
The most beautiful person you've ever met. 
See how the light plays around her.   
How it shines and passes through her short dark hair, 
how her face is shaded from its glare.

Along the hills far from her still, 
yet with its rays streaming through the dirty glass in a flickering embrace. 
You want to remember forever, 
how the sun hugged her at the window. 

The same sunrise, 
the same sunset. 
The same sun you've stared at hundreds of times at windows, 
with your knuckles pressed to your face and your eyes scanning the hilltops passing by, 
thinking of her in a spiral of thoughts. 

The same sun you see holds itself above her. 
In the far hills it might remain, 
like the aching boy who couldn’t let himself be in love with the only one he thinks about.  
Yet like that boy's love, 
that day it reached out. 
Its gleaming edges shining through the window to her, 
and his eyes looking on in wonder. 

The light brought your eyes to her then, her eyes closed in sleep.
You saw her and all you could think, 
all you could feel, 
was that everything would be okay.

Maybe you'll write a poem for her about that moment. 
That time and trip,  
when even golden sunrise with its jealous amber facade, 
knew for once of something more beautiful than it.  

Maybe you’ll end that poem with something sappy (though it's already full of that!), 
yet only true -
